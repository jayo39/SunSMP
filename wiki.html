Welcome to SunSMP

Last updated: Oct 2, 2026

What is SunSMP?

We are a dedicated, grief-free survival community focused on collaborative gameplay, custom economies, and player interaction. We've taken the vanilla Minecraft experience and upgraded it with features designed for builders and adventurers alike.

In this brand-new server, you can make friends, build your dream base, and enjoy optional PvP without ever losing your items—plus so much more!

Main Commands

Command Description
/spawn Teleport to SunSMP spawn
/rtp Random teleport to wilderness
/back Return to your previous location
/sethome <name> Save your current location
/home <name> Teleport to a saved home
/tpa <username> Request to teleport to another player
/tpahere <username> Request another player to teleport to your location
/shop Access the in-game shop to purchase items
/pshop Teleport to player shops to buy and sell items

Server Features

Economy

Discover countless custom ways to make and spend money! Trade with players at the public mall, invest your wealth in custom Bling jewelry, or visit the daily Black Market.

Land Claiming

Claim your land using a golden shovel! Right-click two opposite corners to claim a rectangular area, allowing you to build in peace without the worry of griefing.

Custom Furniture & Items

Standard blocks not enough? We've added custom decorative furniture and items so you can detail your builds exactly how you want.

Dungeons

Looking for a real challenge? Our massive dungeons are designed to test your limits and give you a true late-game purpose. Explore sprawling arenas packed with actual puzzles, animated parkour, and strategic custom boss fights that provide endless endgame progression.

PyroFishing

Take your fishing to the next level! Catch unique custom fish, participate in server-wide fishing tournaments, and unlock powerful augments to permanently upgrade your fishing rod while building a diverse collection of rare catches.

mcMMO

Level up your basic survival skills just by playing the game. Perform everyday Minecraft actions to gain experience and unlock powerful new abilities as you progress.

Seasonal Events

We run regular events throughout the year where you can access special limited-time dungeons and unlock exclusive, themed furniture.
